Pair the remote

Needed after replacing the ventilation unit or resetting the remote.

  1. Unplug the ventilation unit for 5 seconds, then plug it back in.
  2. Within 3 minutes, tap Pair: the ESP holds 1 and Auto together for 5 seconds.
  3. The remote's LED blinks when it is paired. If not, start again from step 1.

Reset the remote

Holds Default and Timer together for 4 seconds, which puts the remote back to its factory settings. It may then have to be paired again.
